ankifu Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile app ankifu (Android package com.ankifu.app) handles information.

Summary

ankifu does not require an account and does not send your personal data to our servers. Quiz progress, settings, and imported kifu data are stored only on your device.

Information we store on your device

The app may store locally on your device:

  • App settings (for example theme, retries, orientation)
  • Imported or bundled kifu collections and related quiz metadata
  • Attempt history and spaced-repetition progress

This information stays on your device (local storage such as AsyncStorage / app documents). It is not uploaded to ankifu or phxshogi.com by the app.

Files you import

If you import a folder of .kif files, those files are read with your permission and stored locally in the app for training. We do not transmit those files to a server as part of normal app use.

Data we do not collect

  • No login or account registration
  • No cloud sync of your collections or stats
  • No advertising identifiers collected by ankifu for ads
  • We do not sell your personal information

Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process device and store-related information under Google’s own policies when you download or update the app.

Children

ankifu is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear app data or uninstall ankifu to remove locally stored data from your device.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@phxshogi.com (or your preferred public contact on phxshogi.com).
